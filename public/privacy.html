POLICY.

This policy explains what information may be collected when you use this website or contact Arayan Lama, and how it is used.

1. Who this policy covers

This website is operated by Arayan Lama (“I”, “me” or “my”). For privacy questions, requests or concerns, email arayanlama19@gmail.com.

2. Information you choose to provide

If you use the project enquiry form or contact me directly, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, project requirements, service selection, budget range, deadline and reference links, along with anything else you include in your message.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers or other sensitive information through the enquiry form.

3. Why the information is used

Your information is used to respond to enquiries, discuss and scope potential projects, prepare estimates, communicate about work, deliver agreed services, maintain relevant business records and address follow-up questions or disputes. I do not use enquiry details to send unrelated marketing newsletters.

4. Enquiry form and service providers

The contact form is processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service, and is configured to forward submissions to arayanlama19@gmail.com. Information you submit is handled by that provider as part of delivering the message and may be processed under its own terms and privacy practices. You can also contact me directly by email or phone instead of using the form.

Email, hosting and website infrastructure providers may process limited information as needed to deliver their services.

5. Embedded media and external links

Some work samples may load YouTube video players when you open a timeline chapter. Social, streaming and map links may take you to third-party websites. Those services may collect information or use cookies under their own policies. I do not control their data practices. You can choose not to play embedded media and visit external links only when you want to.

6. Cookies and analytics

This website is not intentionally configured with advertising cookies or an analytics profile. Third-party media or services may use their own cookies or similar technologies when loaded. Your browser settings can help you manage cookies and external content.

7. Sharing and retention

I do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the website, receive enquiries or provide a service, or where disclosure is required by law. Enquiry correspondence is kept only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a project, maintain appropriate records or resolve a dispute, subject to applicable legal requirements.

8. Your choices and requests

You may ask me to correct or delete personal information you have sent, or withdraw permission for further use where applicable. Some records may need to be retained for legal, accounting or dispute-resolution purposes. To make a request, email arayanlama19@gmail.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line. I may need to verify that the request is yours.

9. Security and changes

I take reasonable practical steps to limit access to enquiry information, but no website, email system or online transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. This policy may be updated as the website or its services change. The revision date above indicates the latest update.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy or the information you have shared, contact arayanlama19@gmail.com.

This is a plain-language website policy, not a legal opinion. It should be reviewed against the actual services, providers and applicable legal requirements before being relied on as a compliance document.
